## Explicit tested area metric

Every source triangle has `J_t = A_3D(t) / A_BFF_UV(t)`. Source triangles are clipped to the two children using the parent rectangular Ω chart, and each child is separately flattened by official BFF using `boundary_scale_zero`. A source 14×14 M2D square `Q_i` retains its original Ω footprint. For triangle--tile overlap areas `a_it` calculated **in initial Ω coordinates**, define `m_i = Σ_t(a_it J_t) / Σ_t a_it` and `σ_area(C) = max_{i∈C} m_i / min_{i∈C} m_i`. This is a tile-mean **area ratio**. It is not necessarily the conformal scale factor intended by the authors in Sec.4.5 or a fabrication guarantee.

All runs avoid silently discarding incompletely measured tiles and preserve original Ω grid/tile identifiers. Topological splits use independent vertex IDs across cut seams; zero shared IDs across disconnected pieces are recorded. The independent free boundary BFF coordinates **never replace** original manufacturing coordinates.

### Robustness

Full `--sensitivity` 5 grid resolutions ×4 smoothing rings = 20 runs:
- Grid 12,13,14: 12/12 runs yield **1 cut** and σ_area<=2.
- Grid 15,16: 8/8 runs yield **2 cuts** and σ_area<=2.
- Thus **12/20**, not 18/20, yield one cut under the present explicit new discretization (the earlier 18/20 figure belonged to the missing patch implementation).

Single-corner ±4 boundary-vertex perturbations:
- **7/8 yield one cut**, one (corner index 1, −4 boundary vertices) yields **two cuts**.

Curvature smoothing ring counts 2,3,5,8 yielded equal cut *counts* at fixed Grid sizes; this does **not** prove identical peak/cut locations on every Grid.

## Interpretation / unresolved

1. **The proposed objective can produce exactly one paper-like Lilium cut**, independently of the unseen v2 patch, with a fixed original 14×14 grid.
2. **It is resolution and corner-sensitive.** This one-cut result is not generally robust for all discretizations.
3. **The same threshold admits multiple candidate cut lines**, so the choice of high-curvature point is not uniquely implied by the CSF threshold.
4. **The metric is not a proven physical auxetic feasibility test.** No direct evidence is established that the initial Ω manufacturing K2D/T2D linkage can open into the 3D shape without collision or hinge-constraint violation. The reference paper's 193-vs-196 tile discrepancy remains.
5. **The first attached note's parent≤product(children) argument is invalid.** Different constant J scales in the two children are a direct mathematical counterexample, even for a fixed UV mapping.
6. Other published meshes and a genuine **unmodified Paper mode baseline** need independent evidence; do not report those as completed until actual outputs are inspected.
